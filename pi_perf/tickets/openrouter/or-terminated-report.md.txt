# Report: Mid-stream generation terminations on `deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash-0731`

**Account:** workspace `3db9a730-07db-5f45-b546-6f017d3c6446` (app_id 2853275, client `pi`)
**Window:** 2026-08-29, ~17:00–21:00 UTC
**Symptom:** OpenAI-compatible streaming requests accepted with HTTP 200, then the SSE
connection is cut mid-stream with no error frame. Client (Node/undici) sees a bare
`TypeError: terminated`; `finish_reason` never arrives. Retries reproduce at high rates.

## Evidence summary

Queried `GET /api/v1/generation?id=...` for **138** failed turns captured by our client
(120 resolvable; 18 return "Generation not found" — likely dropped pre-logging).

### 1. Deaths are mid-stream, not rejections

Of 120 resolvable failed generations:

| finish_reason | native | cancelled | count |
|---|---|---|---|
| `null` | `null` | `true` | 101 |
| `tool_calls` | `tool_calls` | `false` | 19 |

- The 101: upstream generated a median of **1,682 completion tokens** (min 1, max 5,455,
  all reasoning tokens), then the stream was cut with **no finish reason**. Usage reported
  as 0 (not billed).
- The 19: upstream **completed** with `finish_reason: tool_calls`, but the client socket
  was closed before the final frames arrived (tail-cut after completion).
- 0 of 120 died before the first token — every one was accepted and actively generating.

### 2. Deaths cluster just under 30 seconds

`generation_time` distribution of the 101 mid-stream deaths (5s buckets):

```
0-5s:5  5-10s:4  10-15s:6  15-20s:5  20-25s:16  25-30s:65  >30s:0
```

- **33% land in [29.0s, 30.0s)**; **none exceed 30s** — consistent with a hard ~30s
  cutoff (total-time or no-data watchdog) somewhere in the OpenRouter → upstream path.
- The 19 post-completion tail-cuts do run longer (up to 59s), i.e. they escaped the cutoff
  because upstream had already finished.

### 3. One subprovider (Relace) is massively over-represented

Control group: 150 successful generations sampled from the same window (of 348 total),
same model, same client. The "death rate" column is
`dead / (ok_sampled + dead)` — the fraction of that provider's served requests that
died mid-stream.

| provider | ok (sample) | dead | served | death rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Relace** | 46 | 80 | 126 | **≈63%** (43% if ok scaled ×2.32 to full window) |
| Upstage | 0 | 7 | 7 | 100% (n=7, low traffic) |
| Modal | 0 | 6 | 6 | 100% (n=6, low traffic) |
| Z.AI | 0 | 3 | 3 | 100% (n=3, low traffic) |
| Sail Research | 0 | 1 | 1 | 100% (n=1) |
| DigitalOcean | 6 | 2 | 8 | 25% |
| OpenAI | 6 | 1 | 7 | 14% |
| Baidu | 20 | 1 | 21 | 5% |
| Poolside | 61 | 0 | 61 | **0%** |
| Novita | 11 | 0 | 11 | **0%** |

Equivalently, as traffic shares: Relace carried ~31% of successful traffic but
~79% of mid-stream deaths. Poolside and Novita served substantial traffic with
**zero** deaths. The 100%-rate providers served few requests (likely only routed when
preferred providers were busy), so their rates are noisy but consistently bad.

### 4. Uptime telemetry does not reflect this failure mode

Your public endpoints API showed Relace at **99.21% uptime (1d) / 99.69% (30m)** for this
model while it was killing ~2 of every 3 streams it served for us. Mid-stream
terminations are recorded as `cancelled: true, usage: 0` and appear not to count against
endpoint uptime. Clients cannot see this failure mode from any public metric.

## Asks

1. Investigate the Relace endpoint for `deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash-20260731` — high
   mid-stream termination rate (~63% in our sample), with generation_time clustering
   under a ~30s wall.
2. Confirm whether a ~30s total-time or no-data watchdog exists in the OpenRouter proxy
   path (or is inherited from upstream LBs) and whether it can be raised for
   long-reasoning streams.
3. Count mid-stream terminations (`cancelled + no finish_reason`) in endpoint
   uptime/health metrics, or expose them per endpoint — currently they are invisible, so
   `provider.order`/`only` routing cannot be configured sensibly.
4. Clarify the 19 cases where upstream completed (`finish_reason: tool_calls`) but the
   client socket was closed before the stream tail.
